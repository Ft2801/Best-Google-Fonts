# Code of Conduct

## Commitment

The Best Google Fonts project aims to provide a respectful, constructive, and harassment-free environment for everyone who participates.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully and focus on the project.
- Give and receive technical feedback in good faith.
- Respect different viewpoints and levels of experience.
- Correct mistakes openly and constructively.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or deliberately inflammatory comments.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues or pull requests.

## Enforcement

The project owner may edit, remove, or reject contributions and comments that violate this policy. Serious or repeated violations may result in exclusion from project spaces.

Conduct concerns should be reported privately through the contact options available on the [Fabio Tempera GitHub profile](https://github.com/Ft2801).
